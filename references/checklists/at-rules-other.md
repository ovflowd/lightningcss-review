# Checklist: at-rules-other (prefix ATRULE)
Covers `src/rules/page.rs`, `counter_style.rs`, `namespace.rs`, `viewport.rs`, `document.rs` (@-moz-document), `position_try.rs`, `unknown.rs`, `view_transition.rs` (shared with transition-animation), their prelude arms in `src/parser.rs` (`AtRulePrelude` `:199`, `allowed_in_style_rule` `:262`, `NestedRuleParser::parse_prelude` `:572`, placement check `:744`), the unknown-rule paths of nesting lowering (`src/rules/style.rs`) and napi custom at-rules (`napi/src/at_rule_parser.rs`). Spec, invariants (INV-PAGE-*, INV-POS-004, INV-COUNTER-001, INV-NS-001, INV-VT-004) and master status: `references/spec/layout.md` §10.
Top failure modes: (1) unknown at-rules hoisted out of their style rule or printed without its selector when nesting is lowered (#1232, #896); (2) at-rules accepted or rejected in the wrong context because a prelude arm skips the shared check (#962, #968), and errorRecovery dropping more than the bad rule (#1358); (3) raw identifier printing that breaks re-parsing (`@page \31 a` → `@page 1a`, `@a\7d z` → `@a}z`, EM-04).
Answer FAIL only when head introduces or widens a violation; an `open:` defect that base already has is PASS with an unchanged case as evidence, and a PR that fixes one is credited (A2). Probe ids are curated `corpus/probes/<area>/` ids. The oracle has no print media and no anchor layout (L5, L6): @page, @position-try, @counter-style and @view-transition need output assertions, R2 and parse-error checks.
