# Selectors checklist (prefix SEL)
Covers `src/selector.rs` (pseudo tables, serializer, `is_compatible`, prefixes, downleveling), `selectors/` (`parcel_selectors`: parser state, specificity, serde) and the selector paths of `src/rules/style.rs` (`update_prefix`, per-prefix printing) and `src/rules/mod.rs:757-1108` (split, `:is()` wrap, merge). Facts, INV-SEL ids, anchors and verified master defects: `references/spec/selectors.md`; anchors below are `https://drafts.csswg.org/<spec>/#<anchor>`.
Top failure modes: (1) merging or splitting puts an engine-invalid selector into a list or loses split-off selectors (#548, #1260, #1310, S2); (2) `:is()` wrapping, unwrapping or `:-webkit-any()` lowering changes validity or specificity (#536, #345, #997, #1324); (3) making a pseudo known rejects valid continuations or lacks compat data (#1303, #1244, #888).
`open:` marks a defect master already has: answer FAIL only if head is worse than base, extends it to new inputs, or claims the fix while the probe still fails; a real fix is PASS (credited through A2). `fixed:` names past regressions the item guards.
