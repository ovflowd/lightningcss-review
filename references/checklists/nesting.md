# nesting checklist (NEST)
Covers nested-rule parsing in `src/parser.rs` (`:462-555`, `:987-1094`), `src/rules/nesting.rs`, nesting lowering at print time in `src/rules/style.rs` (`:213-333`), `serialize_nesting` and the `&` swaps in `src/selector.rs` (`:1455-1565`, `:1742-1773`), and nested lists in `CssRuleList::minify`. Facts, INV-NEST ids, defects D8/D9: `references/spec/cascade-conditional.md` §8, lowering §11.
Top failure modes: (1) side rules of declarations after a nested rule dropped or leaked to a sibling (#1023, #1049, #1246, #1381, all from 6c465c1 v1.30.0); (2) invalid minified output when nesting is preserved (#989 family); (3) pseudo-element parents and `:is()`/`&` lowering that changes what matches or its specificity (#982, #202, #1073, #1189).
`[open …]` in a verify line = master already fails: answer FAIL only if head is worse than base, extends the defect to new inputs, or claims a fix its probe does not confirm; a real fix is PASS (credit via A2). `[guard …]` = master passes; a regression there is a FAIL.
